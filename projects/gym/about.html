ABOUT IRONPEAK FITNESS

Home > About Us

Built by Athletes, For Athletes

Founded in 2016 by former national-level powerlifter Karan Joshi, IronPeak Fitness was born out of a simple belief — that every person deserves access to elite-level training. What started as a 2,000 sq ft space with a handful of members has grown into Pune's most trusted fitness destination, serving 500+ members and counting.

Today, we fuse world-class coaching, premium equipment, and an unbreakable sense of community to help you redefine what's possible — on and off the gym floor.

Our Mission

To empower every individual to reach their peak physical and mental potential through expert guidance, premium facilities, and an unbreakable community.

Our Vision

To become India's most respected fitness brand — one city at a time.

Meet the Founder

Karan Joshi

Founder & Head of Performance

Former national powerlifting champion, NSCA-certified trainer with 15+ years in the fitness industry. Karan built IronPeak from the ground up with one goal — to raise the standard of fitness culture in India.

Under his leadership, IronPeak champions discipline, longevity, and performance that lasts — never quick fixes or empty promises.
